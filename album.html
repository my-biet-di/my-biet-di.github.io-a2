Love & Letter

Love & Letter is their debut (first) studio album, released on April 25, 2016, through Pledis Entertainment. The album includes 10 tracks that focus on themes of first love experiences. Love & Letter has a youthful and energetic style, and the members also participated in writing and co-composing for almost every track. During the album's promotion, the group won their first two Show Champion awards with the song "Pretty U," which is also the album's title track. With this song, they also charted at number 3 on Billboard's World Digital Songs chart, achieving their highest ranking at the time.

TEEN, Age

TEEN, Age is their second studio album, released on November 6, 2017. The album features many songs with the number 13, coinciding with the group's number of members, reaching number 13 on the Billboard charts. This album is the third chapter of their “2017 Seventeen Project,” which started with the release of Al1 in May of the same year. Because of this, many assumed a dark concept would be the main theme for this album, but that wasn't the case. “Clap,” with its fast, bright rhythm and catchy lyrics, perfectly captures the youthful, playful yet mature side of teenagers growing up.

An Ode

An Ode is the group's third album, released on September 16, 2019. It's considered a major turning point in their career. Unlike their previous studio albums with their cheerful style, this album has a darker and more mature feel. Seventeen aims to convey a message about the "fear" that everyone experiences, using unique methods only Seventeen can do. The title track, "Fear," with its powerful yet heavy bass, expresses this shared fear.

Face the sun

This is Seventeen's fourth album, released on May 27, 2022, one day after the group's 7th anniversary. This album brings a new energy, unlike previous Seventeen albums, and explores a deeper aspect of anxieties encountered while pursuing dreams. It's an album with diverse emotions; each song has a different vibe but still retains the group's unique style. "Hot," with its explosive title track, has a slightly rebellious vibe.

Happy BurstDay

This is their 5th album, released on May 26, 2025, to celebrate their 10th anniversary. The album is titled "Happy Burstday" instead of "Happy Birthday" as usual because the group thought it would be like an explosive burst of energy sent to their fans. This is a special album, as each member has a solo track, even those who were serving in the military at the time. This album captures all the energy the group has shown over the past 10 years, but it's not as youthful as when they first debuted; instead, it's more mature and experienced. With "Happy Burstday," Seventeen wants to express their gratitude to their fans and open a promising new chapter.
