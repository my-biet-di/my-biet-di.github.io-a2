SEVENTEEN

Seventeen is a South Korean boy group formed by Pledis Entertainment, now a subsidiary of HYBE, that debuted on May 26, 2015. The group consists of 13 members: S.Coups, Jeonghan, Joshua, Jun, Hoshi, Wonwoo, Woozi, The8, DK, Mingyu, Seungkwan, Vernon, and Dino. The name "17" comes from the fact that the group has 13 members, divided into 3 smaller units (vocal, hip hop, and performance), forming one large group; adding up the numbers in each unit results in 17.
The group is known for its distinctive, cheerful, and energetic music, along with lyrics that are distinctly youthful and not forced into adulthood. They are also known for being self-producing, with most of their songs being written and choreographed by the members themselves.
